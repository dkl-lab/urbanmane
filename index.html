Mane District operates a mobile hair and beauty booking platform connecting clients with vetted, independent professionals across the UK. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

  • Clients booking: name, phone, service, date/time, and verified address including postcode.
  • Professionals applying: name, email, phone, city, specialty, experience, and DBS certificate information.

Hub 08 · Help Centre

Answers to common questions.

Can't find what you need? Email urbanmane@protonmail.com

How do I book a stylist?

Go to Book a Stylist, choose your service, date, time, street address and postcode, and submit. We'll confirm your stylist match by email.

Why do you need my postcode?

Your postcode is verified against the official UK postcode database so we can assign the nearest available professional. We never share your address with anyone other than your confirmed stylist.

Is payment taken upfront?

No payment is required to submit a booking. You can optionally secure your slot with a refundable £15 deposit via Stripe.

Are stylists background-checked?

Yes. Every professional holds a valid Enhanced DBS certificate, personally reviewed by our team before they can accept bookings.

Which cities do you operate in?

We are currently live in London and Manchester, and accepting booking requests from anywhere in the UK. Submit your request with your postcode and we will confirm the nearest available professional in your area.

How do I become a stylist on the platform?

Visit Apply to Join and submit your details. Our team will follow up to begin vetting and DBS verification.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes — email urbanmane@protonmail.com with your booking details as early as possible. Deposits are refundable with 24 hours notice.
